I've wanted a place of my own on the internet for a while: somewhere to think out loud about the
systems I build, keep notes I'll actually reread, and write about the things that don't fit on a
résumé.

## What you'll find here

- **Engineering notes** — distributed systems, databases, and the infrastructure underneath.
- **Things I'm learning** — half-formed ideas are welcome.
- **Detours** — soccer, films, and a novel I'm slowly writing.

## Why write at all

Writing forces the fuzzy parts of an idea into the open. If a paragraph won't hold together, I
usually didn't understand the thing as well as I thought. That's the whole point.
