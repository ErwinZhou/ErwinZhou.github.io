## Overview

A reverse-mode automatic differentiation engine, implemented from scratch on top of NumPy. This
is the same core mechanism — building a computational graph of tensor operations and running
backpropagation over it — that underlies frameworks like PyTorch and TensorFlow. I used the
finished engine to train a logistic regression classifier end-to-end, verifying that gradients
computed through the graph actually drive a real model to converge.
