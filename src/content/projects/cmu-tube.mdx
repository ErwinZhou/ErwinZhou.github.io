## Overview

Implemented and tuned an Adaptive Bitrate (ABR) algorithm for video streaming, targeting realistic
last-mile network conditions rather than a clean, high-bandwidth link. The work sat on top of a
DASH-based video streaming testbed using the dash.js player, containerized with Docker.
